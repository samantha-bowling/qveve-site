Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

At QVEVE, your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, and don't collect, when you visit qveve.music.

1. Information we collect

This site is a static informational page. It has no accounts, no signup forms, and no comment or chat features. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personal information through this site.

We may use aggregate, cookie-free analytics to understand roughly how many people visit and which pages they view. This kind of analytics does not identify you individually.

We do not knowingly collect any information from minors under 13.

2. Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own.

3. Third-party services

A few outside services are involved in serving this site:

  • Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and standard browser information, subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Links. We link to other sites, including our interactive demo at demo.qveve.music, Instagram, and PNWK Records. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

We will never sell or rent your data to anyone.

4. The QVEVE platform

This policy covers the qveve.music website only. The QVEVE streaming platform and its demo will have their own privacy policy covering accounts, listening data, ENQORE, and everything else that comes with using the product.

5. Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information here, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you have contacted us by email and would like that correspondence deleted, just ask.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add features that collect information, such as a mailing list, we'll update this policy and the effective date before they go live.

7. Contact us

Questions? Reach out at samantha@qveve.music.
